# Exyora AI: Autonomous Cross-Border & Student Finance Assistant
> Submitted for the **2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (FinTechathon)** — International Track (Topic C: Cross-Border & Student Finance Assistant)[cite: 1, 2].

## 🚀 Executive Summary
Exyora AI is an advanced, autonomous multi-agent financial assistant engineered specifically to address the complex cross-border financial friction points faced by international students and global migrants[cite: 1, 2]. By integrating state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) with secure multi-agent orchestration, open banking APIs, and decentralized routing protocols, Exyora AI delivers seamless real-time execution for international remittances, automated tuition escrow management, multi-currency cash flow optimization, and cross-border credit building without traditional collateral requirements.

---

## 🧠 Core Architecture & Proprietary Algorithms
Exyora AI features three core proprietary algorithms optimized for accuracy, speed, and fairness:
1. **DFSO-R (Dynamic Fee & Speed-Optimized Remittance Routing Algorithm):** Continuously scrapes live mid-market exchange rates, flat fees, percentage markups, and delivery latencies to compute the cheapest and fastest corridor for tuition payments.
2. **FC-CSM (Federated Cross-Border Credit Scoring Model):** Computes portable alternative credit scores using encrypted home-country financial telemetry securely under GDPR compliance.
3. **Multi-Currency Liquidity & FX Risk Hedging Predictor:** Uses LSTM time-series forecasting models to predict short-term currency fluctuations and advise students on optimal conversion timings.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & System Architecture
* **Frontend:** Swiss Editorial Brutalist & Glassmorphism UI (HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JS)
